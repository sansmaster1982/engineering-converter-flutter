Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Overview

Engineering Calculator HVAC ("the App") is a free utility application for unit conversion and HVAC engineering calculations. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The App:

  • Does not require registration or login
  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or tracking tools
  • Does not contain advertisements
  • Does not make network requests — works entirely offline

Data Storage

The App stores only your language preference locally on your device. No data is transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
